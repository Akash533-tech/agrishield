# Three-minute demo

1. **0:00–0:30 — Mission.** Open the landing page. Explain that AgroShield combines parametric protection with separately verified environmental activity. Call out sample/test labels.
2. **0:30–1:05 — Farmer protection.** Launch the app, complete role-aware onboarding, connect MetaMask on chain `10143`, claim test aUSD, deposit test pool liquidity from Underwriter, then open Insurance. Adjust coverage and approve the two real test-token/policy transactions. Open the confirmed receipt in Monad Explorer.
3. **1:05–1:35 — Evidence to units.** Open Evidence, pin a public demo image and metadata through Pinata, and register the returned CID/hash on Monad. Use three staked verifier wallets to reach quorum, then issue a verified-only demo batch. Units are explicitly demos—not certified offsets.
4. **1:35–2:15 — Capital safety.** Open Underwriter and Climate lab. Select Drought, vary covered farmers/liquidity, and show payouts, remaining capital, and solvency recompute immediately. Explain reserved capital cannot be withdrawn.
5. **2:15–2:45 — Security.** Show tests covering unauthorized/stale/replayed settlement, insolvency, reserved withdrawals, duplicate issuance, burn-based retirement, vote replay, and appeal-delayed slashing.
6. **2:45–3:00 — Testnet.** List units, switch to a buyer wallet, approve test aUSD and purchase atomically, then permanently retire units and open the receipt. Show the persisted deployment manifest and real Pinata CID.
